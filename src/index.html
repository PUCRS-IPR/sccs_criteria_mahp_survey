Pairwise Judgment Scale Guide

In the AHP / Fuzzy-AHP method, experts perform 1-on-1 comparisons between pairs of criteria or sub-criteria. The evaluation is conducted in two sequential steps:

1. Choice of Dominant Criterion:

Indicate which of the two elements under evaluation has a greater impact or relevance to the objective.

2. Intensity of Preference (Verbal Scale):
  • Equal importance: Both criteria contribute equally to the objective.
  • Moderately superior: Experience and judgment slightly favor one criterion over the other.
  • Superior: Experience and judgment strongly favor one criterion over the other.
  • Very superior: One criterion is very strongly favored, with practical, demonstrable dominance.
  • Extremely superior: The evidence favoring one criterion over another is of the highest possible order of affirmation.
